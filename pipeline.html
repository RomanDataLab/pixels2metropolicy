Decision pipeline

The tool end-to-end, for metropolitan urban management: satellite segmentation and socio-economic analysis run in parallel, land on the same community polygon, get ranked by domain, and flow through selection, participation and a policy kit — with an after-action audit that re-scores the polygon and closes the loop. Human decides at stage 3; the tool prioritizes, never decides. The unit throughout is the reconciled community polygon (CMT_ID), the join of BMA boundaries and the NHA register.

Stage 1A — Segmentation: two outputs, one honest gate

  • Corroboration: roof density, growth and fire-scar indicators over registered communities — the pixels agree or disagree with the register.
  • Screening queue: 244 review areas city-wide — 154 with no register overlap (inspect evidence beyond the register), 90 with overlapping boundaries to reconcile. A survey queue, not a claim of new settlements.
  • Honest gate: spatial AUC 0.733 sits below our own pre-set classification gate, so model output is a screening signal — where to send enumerators — never a settlement verdict. The map does not establish poverty, tenure or legality.
  • Evidence: Gen 1 viewer · Gen 2 viewer.

Cross-source agreement raises the confidence flag; disagreement lowers it and routes the polygon to field enumeration. The evaluation is explicit: social data is a signal, not truth. Match tiers T1–T4 carry explicit confidence (0.9 / 0.7 / 0.4 / 0.3) — see the social pulse page.

Stage 2 — Domain ranking

Per-community sub-scores per domain, critical conditions highlighted, composite 0–100 with trend arrow and confidence flag:

fire risk flood exposure service backlog complaint intensity street economy tenure pressure

Publication guardrail: scores are an internal investment-priority tool — published only as budget tiers, never a ranked league table, with data-blindness flags showing who the sources miss.

Stage 3 — Selection

The governance body (BMA district office, NHA, CODI) selects which neighborhoods to react to now, within budget tiers. Ranking prioritizes; the human decides. Selection is logged with the score snapshot that justified it.

Stage 4 — Participation

Participation techniques are chosen from the classified pool — matched to the kit and to community capacity: enumeration before engineering works, collective negotiation before tenure instruments. Classes 3–5 are the operable pool; failure modes from 49 global cases constrain the selector. See the participation techniques page.

Stage 5 — Policy kit dispatch

Score profile triggers a kit via an explicit dispatch table. Every terminal state is a funded programme or an enumeration visit — never "monitor". Kit detail on the proposal page.

After delivery: an after-action THEOS-2 audit re-runs the same sensor that screened the area, the polygon is re-scored, and the ranking updates — the loop closes on evidence, not on completion reports. The governor-visit protocol carries the same discipline: structured enumeration visits, findings written back within 48 hours.
