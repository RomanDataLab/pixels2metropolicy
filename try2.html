Try 2 — SAM-hybrid roof segmentation

THEOS-2 0.5 m · 2 AOIs near Pathum Thani · 2026-09-17

Method

Try 1 flagged "informal roof material" anywhere the spectral rules fired — including roads and bare lots. Try 2 adds HQ-SAM (Segment Anything, high-quality weights, via the open-source segment-geospatial package): its automatic instance raster acts as a gate — a roof only counts where SAM sees a discrete structure. Material (zinc / tarpaulin) is still classified by the spectral rules; individual roofs are recovered by connected components inside the gate.

The try-2 mask is a strict subset of try 1's: 90–93% of the removed area was cut purely by the SAM gate (no structure detected there), consistent with try 1's known false positives on roads and bare lots. Roof-size distribution is preserved.
No ground truth yet — the 22–32% area removal cannot be fully split into false-positive removal vs missed roofs. Treat the density grid change with care until a labeled-sample audit. A pure one-polygon-per-SAM-object mode (no spectral gate) was also tested and rejected: it collapsed to 79/127 roofs (under-recall on dense shack clusters).
