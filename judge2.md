# Judging Criteria — Round 2 (Final)

Source: `geohackathon_information.html`, "Judging Criteria" tab.

**Final Round — 31 October 2026** (Grand Finale, Thailand Space Expo 2026)

| Criterion | Weight | What is evaluated |
|---|---|---|
| Geo Intelligence Quality | 40% | Does the output produce a genuine spatial insight meaningful to decision-makers? Is it specific to the regional context and practically applicable? |
| GeoAI Methodology | 35% | Is the AI/ML approach appropriate and credible? Is the Data Pipeline clearly explained? Are data limitations and uncertainty acknowledged? |
| Communication and Impact | 25% | Can the team explain their work to a non-technical audience? Is the Visualisation compelling and decision-supporting? Is there a clear Call to Action? |
