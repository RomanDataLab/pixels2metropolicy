    09Data analytics · identification

    What the numbers say

    Density dominates. Four of the five strongest separators are density measured at different radii, and the fifth is a model input. None of them establishes settlement status, which is why field validation comes next.

    BANGKOK · BIRD'S-EYE EVIDENCE SCENE

    The most vulnerable are hit first. And reached last.

    กลุ่มเปราะบางคือคนแรกที่เจ็บเสมอ และมักเป็นคนสุดท้ายที่ความช่วยเหลือไปถึง

    Fire, flood and eviction reach informal communities before anyone else, and support reaches them last because the city often cannot see them in its data. We are building a policy toolkit and methodology that lets government identify urbanisation and settlement change as it happens, then match each area with the response it actually needs.

    15Two scales of evidence

    From zones to roofs

    We work at two scales because they answer different questions. The city scale finds rough zones worth a closer look. The roof scale traces real building outlines inside a community or neighbourhood. Both need field validation before anyone acts on them.

    Hover an image to magnify · click to open full size

    FIELD VALIDATION REQUIRED · BOTH SCALES

    Zones and roof outlines are both model output. Neither establishes who lives there, tenure or legality. Every area is checked on the ground with the community before it informs a decision.

    16Scaling to the metropolis

    19Research methodology

    How the evidence is built

    Community records, building evidence and satellite imagery describe the same city in different ways. The method brings them together and keeps their limits beside the map.

    QResearch question

    • Where do records and building evidence disagree in Bangkok?
    • Which of those places should a field team check first, and why?
    • Does screening add information beyond building density alone?

    DData

    • BMA community polygons (1,993; 626 congested)
    • NHA low-income locations (1,588) and Baan Mankong projects (177)
    • Fire-risk assessments 2568; flood-wall community list
    • Open building footprints (1.80 M); THEOS-2 0.5 m imagery

    MMethods and diagnostics

    • Cross-register spatial joins with dated sources
    • 18 morphology descriptors on 40 frames, 28,950 buildings
    • Density dominates: building count and inside density correlate at 0.98
    • Spatial cross-validation by district (AUC 0.733)

    VValidation plan and ethics

    • 300 review units: 120 flagged, 120 density-matched, 60 registered
    • Dated imagery ≤ 1 m, three independent reviewers, stop if kappa < 0.40
    • Community and socio-economic evidence before any policy use
    • No claim of poverty, tenure or legality from imagery
