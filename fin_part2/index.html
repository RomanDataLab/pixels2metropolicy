METROPOLITAN PLANNINGINTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

A platform that joins several engines to predict, plan and carry out urban interventions in Thailand's metropolitan areas, with irregular neighborhoods at the center. Public and corporate decision-makers need it to keep pace with fast-changing cities and suburbs. It reads renewed data from satellites, statistics, public records and social media, finds the irregular neighborhoods most at risk for sustainable city life, and ranks them.
